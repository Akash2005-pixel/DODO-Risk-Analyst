01 • Mental Model & Approach

My Risk Philosophy as an MoR Underwriter

How I approach merchant vetting when balancing aggressive startup growth with card network compliance.

01 / Growth Alignment

Enabling, Not Just Blocking

An entry-level analyst can reject risky applications; a great risk analyst figures out how to approve them safely. For AI-native startups with innovative pricing, my default is to structure risk mitigants (rolling reserves, UX fixes) rather than slam the door.

02 / The Human Element

Beyond Static Databases

Databases check if an LLC exists, but they don't reveal deceptive checkout funnels. I examine live customer journeys, refund flows, and subscription transparency because friendly fraud almost always stems from poor communication at purchase.

03 / Scheme Protection

Guarding Master MIDs

In an MoR model, a single rogue merchant can push our master acquiring MIDs toward Visa VDMP or Mastercard ECM thresholds. I maintain unwavering vigilance on dispute ratios and high-risk mcc code leakage from day one.

Case Study: Evaluating "SynthoVoice AI"

A hyper-realistic onboarding review demonstrating human judgment on an AI voice-cloning SaaS merchant with clean paperwork but dangerous checkout friction.

SynthoVoice AI

AI Text-to-Speech Wrapper • B2C SaaS

Reviewed in 3.4 hrs • Historical Dispute Ratio: 1.4% (Previous Gateway)

CONDITIONAL APPROVAL WITH MITIGANTS

Business Model & Customer Journey Audit

Offers a $1 3-day full access trial for custom AI voice cloning. However, inspecting their checkout flow reveals that trial completion automatically enrolls users into an annual $129 recurring plan with the renewal disclosure buried in 9px footer text. Their historical data shows high friendly-fraud chargebacks due to "subscription misunderstanding."

KYB, Ownership & Technical Verification

Entity & UBO: Delaware LLC registered; UBO identity and passport verified against sanctions lists (Clean).
Website & Funnel: Active AI voice studio web app; clean API uptime, but deceptive auto-renewal trial copy.
Previous Gateway Data: Terminated by prior PSP due to 1.4% dispute ratio stemming entirely from non-fraud recurring bill surprises.

Human Judgment vs. Automated Checklist

A rigid checklist would immediately reject SynthoVoice due to their prior PSP termination and 1.4% dispute ratio. However, applying human risk judgment reveals the product is genuinely useful and loved by users—the churn and disputes are entirely fixable UI/UX compliance issues rather than malicious fraud rings.

Audit-Ready Decision Memo MoR Risk Lead Review

Approved conditionally. By enforcing strict checkout transparency and reserve holds, we capture high-margin AI SaaS GMV while insulating Dodo Payments from card scheme dispute penalties.

Required Mitigants & Conditions
  • 1. Mandatory pre-billing email reminder sent to customers 48 hours before the $1 trial converts to the $129 annual plan.
  • 2. Redesign checkout UI to include an explicit, un-prechecked agreement checkbox directly above the payment CTA.
  • 3. Apply a rolling 30-day reserve hold (15%) until monthly dispute ratios stabilize below 0.50% for two consecutive cycles.

Scheme Standing

Onboarding & Payout Action

Preventive Control

Enable automated pre-dispute alerts (Ethoca / Verifi RDR) and 1-click self-serve cancellation portal.

True Fraud (TC40 / SAFE) Reason Code: 10.4 / 4837

Stolen Credentials & Card Testing

Fraudsters use AI SaaS checkouts with low-ticket trials ($1–$5) or instant API key generation to test stolen card batches before monetizing elsewhere.

• Onboarding Signal: Instant API provisioning without email/domain verification, zero rate-limiting on free/low trials.
• Mitigation: Enforce 3DS2 on high-risk BINs/velocities, block disposable emails, and delay compute/API credits on anomalous cards.
Non-Fraud / Friendly Fraud Reason Code: 13.1 / 13.6 / 4853

Subscription Traps & Descriptor Confusion

Legitimate cardholders dispute charges because an AI tool auto-renewed silently, cancellation required emailing support, or the MoR billing descriptor went unrecognized.

• Onboarding Signal: Pre-checked recurring consent boxes, buried refund policies, or aggressive "limited-time" countdown timers.
• Mitigation: Mandate clear dynamic descriptors (e.g., DODO*MERCHANT), pre-renewal emails, and frictionless refund UX.
